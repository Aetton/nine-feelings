Record an emotion with one tap from your home screen. Nine Feelings is a small, offline journal with a native dark interface.

• Two resizable widgets: 2×2 and 3×3, each with nine emotion buttons.
• Tap the brief confirmation card to add a comment to that exact entry.
• Customize each button's name, symbol and colour.
• Old entries and statistics keep the emotions recorded before a button was changed.
• Browse history and emotion frequencies over 7 days, 30 days or all time.
• Export a readable JSON backup and restore it without duplicate entries.

No accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking or internet permission. Data stays in Android's private app storage. Exported backups are not encrypted; choose their location carefully. Removing the app removes the local diary.

The current interface is in Russian. Android 8.0 or newer is required. This app is a self-observation journal, not a medical device or diagnostic tool. Source code and original assets are available under the MIT License.
